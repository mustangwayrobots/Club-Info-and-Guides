# Battery Safety Acknowledgment Form

**Downers Grove South Robotics Club (Mustang Way Robots)**
Print this page, sign it, and return it to the club advisor. You will not receive a battery, or take a battery-powered project home, until this form is on file.

Goes with: [**SOP-BAT-001 Lithium Battery Safety Policy & SOP**](club_battery_safety_handling_policy.md), version 2.0

---

## Student

I have attended the club battery safety briefing and read the Lithium Battery Safety Policy & SOP. I understand and agree that:

- I will do the 3-point check (look, feel, fit) before every use and every charge.
- I will never puncture, crush, cut, solder to, short or modify a battery.
- I will only charge through the project's USB-C port, on a hard non-flammable surface, **while I am in the room**, and **never overnight**.
- I will stop using and report any battery that is swollen, damaged, hot or smells.
- I will bring damaged or dead batteries back to the club and never throw them in the trash.
- I know what to do in a battery emergency: alert, unplug only if safe, get back, don't touch, tell an adult.
- If I don't follow these rules, I may lose the privilege of using batteries or taking projects home.
