## 2. Respect

* Treat every member, mentor and guest with respect. No harassment, bullying, put-downs or exclusion.
* Help newer members. Everyone started as a beginner.
* Ask before using someone else's project, parts or code.
* Disagree about ideas, not people.

## 3. Safety comes first

* Read and follow every safety document in **Club Info & Guidelines** before using the related equipment.
* You must have a signed **Safety Acknowledgment Form** on file before using batteries, soldering irons, 3D printers or power tools.
* An adult (advisor or approved mentor) must be in the room whenever tools or equipment are in use.
* If you see something unsafe, stop and tell an adult. You will never get in trouble for reporting a safety concern.
* No horseplay in the work area. No food or drinks at soldering or electronics stations.

## 4. Equipment and materials

* Club tools, parts and printers belong to the school. Use them only for club projects unless the advisor says otherwise.
* Report broken or missing equipment right away. Accidents happen; hiding them is the problem.
* Take only the parts you need. Ask before taking expensive or limited parts (microcontrollers, batteries, motors).
* Projects or parts that leave the room must be checked out with the advisor.

## 5. Clean-up

* Stop work **10 minutes** before the end of the meeting to clean up.
* Turn off and unplug soldering irons and hot glue guns. Put them back in their stands to cool.
* Return tools to where they belong. Sweep up wire clippings and plastic scraps.
* Store your project and batteries in the assigned place (see the Battery Safety Policy).

## 6. Online, code and GitHub

* The club's GitHub site is public. **Never post personal information**: no full names, phone numbers, addresses, school ID numbers or photos of students without permission.
* Only change club repos you've been given permission to change.
* Give credit when you use someone else's code or design.
* Keep club chats and comments school-appropriate. School technology rules apply.

## 7. Projects

* No weapons, or anything designed to hurt people or animals. "Combat" robots (like ant-weight bots) are built and run only under club rules, inside an arena, with the advisor present.
* No projects that are offensive, harassing or break school rules.
* When in doubt, ask the advisor before you start.

## 8. If rules aren't followed

Depending on how serious it is:

1. A reminder and a conversation.
2. Losing the use of certain equipment, or the privilege of taking projects home.
3. A call or email home to a parent/guardian.
4. Removal from the club, and referral under the school's discipline process.

Serious safety violations can skip straight to steps 2–4.
