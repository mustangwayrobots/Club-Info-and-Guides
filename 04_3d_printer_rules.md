# 3D Printer Rules

How club members use the 3D printers safely and fairly. Printers have hot parts, moving parts and long print times, so they need a few clear rules.

## 1. Before you print

1. Signed **Safety Acknowledgment Form** on file.
2. Printer training from the advisor or a trained mentor.
3. Your print is **approved** by the advisor (see Section 4).

## 2. The hazards

| Hazard | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Hot nozzle** | Around 200–260 °C (400–500 °F). Touching it causes an instant burn. |
| **Heated bed** | Often 60–110 °C. Hot enough to burn. Stays hot after a print. |
| **Moving parts** | The print head and bed move fast and can pinch fingers. |
| **Fumes and particles** | Melting plastic gives off fumes and tiny particles. PLA gives off the least. |
| **Sharp tools** | Scrapers and hobby knives used to remove and clean prints. |
| **Fire** | Rare, but a printer malfunction can overheat. |

## 3. Safety rules

* **Never reach into a printer while it is moving or hot.** Pause or stop it first and let the nozzle and bed cool.
* Use the club's **PLA** filament. Other materials (ABS, ASA, nylon, etc.) need advisor approval and proper ventilation.
* Keep the printer area clear of paper, cloth and other things that burn.
* Printers stay in a well-ventilated area. Don't sit with your face next to a running printer for long periods.
* **Remove prints carefully**: let the bed cool, then use the scraper pushing **away** from your body and hands. Wear safety glasses when snapping off supports.
* Use hobby knives cutting **away** from yourself, on a cutting mat.
* Don't change printer settings, firmware or hardware without the advisor's okay.
* **Unattended prints:** follow the advisor's rule for this room: _(fill in, e.g. "prints must finish before the building closes" or "no overnight prints")_.

## 4. Fair use and approvals

* Every print needs advisor approval before you start. Have your file, the estimated print time and the amount of filament ready.
* Club projects come first. Personal prints only with advisor permission.
* Long prints (over _(fill in)_ hours) must be scheduled with the advisor.
* **Not allowed:** weapons or weapon parts, anything offensive or against school rules, copyrighted or trademarked designs you don't have the right to make, or anything you'd be uncomfortable showing the principal.
* Check on your print in the first few layers. Stop it if it fails. Don't leave a "spaghetti" mess for the next person.

## 5. Problems

* **Smoke, burning smell, or strange noises:** turn off the printer at the switch or unplug it, step back and tell an adult.
* **Burn:** cool under running water for at least 10–20 minutes and tell an adult. Go to the school nurse.
* **Jammed or broken printer:** stop, don't try to take it apart, put a note on it and tell the advisor.
* **Report** every injury or malfunction to the advisor the same day.

## 6. When you finish

* Remove your print and any scraps from the printer.
* Clean the bed if needed.
* Put tools back and log your print if the club keeps a print log.

## 7. Advisor checklist

- [ ] Printers in a ventilated area, on a stable non-flammable surface, away from exits.
- [ ] Smoke detector working in the room.
- [ ] Scrapers, cutting mats, safety glasses and a first-aid kit nearby.
- [ ] Students trained before first use; training recorded.
- [ ] "Unattended prints" rule decided and filled in above.
- [ ] Print approval process explained to members.
