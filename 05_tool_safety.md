# Hand & Power Tool Safety

Basic rules for the hand tools, power tools and hot glue used to build club projects. Most workshop injuries are cuts, pinches, burns and eye injuries, and almost all are preventable.

## 1. Rules for every tool

* **Safety glasses** when cutting, drilling, clipping, sanding or using any power tool.
* Only use a tool after you've been **shown how** by the advisor or a trained mentor.
* Use the **right tool** for the job. No screwdrivers as chisels or pliers as hammers.
* **Inspect before use.** Don't use a tool with a damaged blade, cracked handle or frayed cord. Report it.
* Keep your work area clear and well lit.
* Tie back long hair; no loose sleeves, dangling jewelry or lanyards near power tools.
* Return tools to where they belong when you're done.

## 2. Cutting tools (hobby knives, box cutters, flush cutters)

* Cut **away** from your body and away from the hand holding the work.
* Use a **cutting mat** and clamp or hold the work flat.
* Retract or cap the blade the moment you finish. Never leave an open blade on the table.
* Dull blades are dangerous. Ask for a new blade, and put used blades in the **sharps container**, never the trash.
* When clipping wire or component leads, point the lead **down** and hold the end so it doesn't fly.

## 3. Power tools (drill, rotary tool, etc.)

* An adult must approve each use.
* **Clamp** the work. Never hold small parts in your hand while drilling or cutting.
* Keep fingers well away from the bit or blade. Let the tool stop completely before setting it down.
* Unplug or remove the battery before changing bits or blades.
* Don't carry a power tool by its cord.
* Wear safety glasses; add a dust mask for sanding.

## 4. Hot glue guns

* The nozzle and fresh glue are hot enough to burn (often around 190 °C / 375 °F).
* Always rest the gun on its **stand** or a heat-resistant mat.
* Don't touch the glue until it has cooled. Don't try to pull hot glue off skin.
* Unplug when finished.

## 5. Electrical work and power supplies

* Unplug or disconnect power before changing wiring.
* Bench power supplies: set the voltage and current limit **before** connecting your circuit. Ask if you're unsure what to use.
* Never work on wall (mains) power wiring. Club projects run on low-voltage batteries and USB only.
* Battery rules are in the **Battery Safety Policy**.

## 6. If someone gets hurt

* **Cut:** apply firm pressure with a clean cloth, tell an adult, go to the school nurse.
* **Burn:** cool under running water for at least 10–20 minutes, tell an adult, go to the nurse.
* **Something in the eye:** don't rub. Use the eyewash station and get an adult.
* **Serious injury:** get an adult immediately and call for help following the school's emergency procedure.
* **Report** every injury, however small, to the advisor the same day.

## 7. Advisor checklist

- [ ] Safety glasses for everyone in the room.
- [ ] First-aid kit stocked; eyewash station location known.
- [ ] Sharps container for used blades.
- [ ] Tools inspected; damaged tools removed from use.
- [ ] Students trained on each power tool before first use; training recorded.
