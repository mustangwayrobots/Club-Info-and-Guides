## Student

I have read every document listed above and attended the club safety briefing. I understand and agree that:

- I will follow the Code of Conduct and all of the safety rules above, at school and at home.
- I will only use equipment I have been trained on, and only when an adult is in the room.
- I will wear safety glasses when the rules say to.
- **Batteries:** I will do the 3-point check before every use, charge only while I'm in the room and never overnight, and return damaged batteries to the club.
- **Soldering:** I will keep the iron in its stand, use the fume extractor, and wash my hands afterward.
- **3D printers:** I will never reach into a moving or hot printer, and I will only print approved files.
- **Tools:** I will cut away from myself and clamp my work.
- I will report every injury, damaged equipment and safety concern to the advisor the same day.
- If I don't follow these rules, I may lose the use of equipment, the privilege of taking projects home, or my place in the club.
